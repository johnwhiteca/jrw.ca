People often confuse letting go with giving up.

They are not the same.

Giving up abandons responsibility.

Letting go begins after responsibility has been honoured.

## Responsibility has a boundary

We are responsible for our effort.

Our choices.

Our integrity.

Our willingness to learn.

We are not responsible for controlling every outcome.

The moment we begin carrying responsibilities that belong elsewhere, frustration grows.

## Freedom begins where control ends

Letting go is not withdrawing from life.

It is recognizing the difference between influence and control.

When we stop trying to control what was never ours, we gain more energy for the things that are.
