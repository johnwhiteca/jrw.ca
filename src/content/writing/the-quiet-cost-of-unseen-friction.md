Most organizations do not fail because of one catastrophic problem.

They slowly accumulate friction.

An unclear process.

An unnecessary approval.

A missing piece of information.

A system that everyone quietly works around.

Each obstacle seems insignificant.

Together they reshape the entire experience of work.

## Friction changes behaviour

People rarely complain about every obstacle.

Instead, they adapt.

Sometimes they stop asking questions.

Sometimes they create their own unofficial process.

Sometimes they simply accept that frustration is normal.

That acceptance is often the greatest hidden cost.

Better systems create better experiences

Removing friction is rarely about working faster.

It is about making it easier for people to do the right thing with confidence.

When systems become easier to navigate, people regain energy that was previously spent overcoming unnecessary obstacles.
